    How to play

    1. When it's your turn, hold the phone so only you can see it, then tap Go!
    2. Describe the big word at the top so the others guess it. You can't say the word itself, part of it, or any danger word in the red list.
    3. No pointing, acting, rhyming ("sounds like…") or spelling.
    4. Someone guessed it? Tap their name (or Got it! in teams). Stuck? Tap Skip. Said a danger word? Be honest and tap Oops!
    5. When the timer runs out, check the words, save the scores and pass the phone on.

    Every player for themselves: the describer gets a point for each word guessed, and so does the person who guessed it.
    Teams: your team gets a point for each word. The other team watches over your shoulder and shouts "Buzz!" if you slip up.
